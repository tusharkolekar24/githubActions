Oracle Inventory

Oracle Inventory is a module within the Oracle E-Business Suite that helps organizations manage their inventory and supply chain operations. It provides tools for tracking inventory levels, managing stock movements, and optimizing inventory replenishment.

With Oracle Inventory, businesses can gain real-time visibility into their inventory levels, reduce carrying costs, and improve order fulfillment. The module also integrates with other Oracle applications such as Purchasing and Order Management to streamline the entire supply chain process.

Overall, Oracle Inventory is a powerful solution for businesses looking to optimize their inventory management and improve their supply chain efficiency.
